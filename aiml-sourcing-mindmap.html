Reading the footprint, not just the title: a title like "MLOps Engineer" is applied inconsistently across companies — the reliable signal is the trail. A GitHub contributor to vLLM or a PyTorch Foundation-hosted project, a Kaggle top-100 finisher, or a named author on an arXiv paper — each is public proof of skill, independent of how any one employer labels the role.
