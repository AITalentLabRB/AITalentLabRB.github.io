What, Where, Gaps & How to Control

1

What Is AI Governance in Recruiting?

Putting clear rules, ownership, testing, and monitoring around any AI tool that influences a candidate or employee.

  • What is the tool doing?
  • What data does it use?
  • Does it recommend, rank, advance, or reject?
  • How was it tested for accuracy and fairness?
  • Where is the human decision-maker?
  • Can a candidate request review or accommodation?
  • How will we know if the tool stops working properly?

The Biggest Industry Gap

We cannot consistently trace how an AI recommendation moves through the CRM & ATS, influences a recruiter, and affects the final candidate decision.

Why this happens in CRM + ATS integrations:

5ATS recommends candidates

A candidate can be disadvantaged at ANY step before the final human decision.

The 6 Most Common Control Gaps

No End-to-End AI Inventory

Companies don't know all the AI features active across CRM, ATS, and other tools.

Poor Data Lineage

Cannot answer: "Why did this candidate get this recommendation?"

Vendor Changes Ignored

Model, data, taxonomy, rules, or defaults change without re-assessment.

Weak Human Oversight

Clicking "Accept" is not meaningful review. Recruiters over-rely on the score.

Bias Measured Only at Final Stage

Problems at sourcing or matching stage stay hidden in overall hiring data.

Fragmented Ownership

No single owner across Recruiting, HR Tech, Legal & Risk, and Vendor.

Top 5 Controls to Start First

  1. Turn off auto-activation of new AI features.
  2. No AI-only rejection for unapproved use cases.
  3. Create a feature-level inventory and data-flow map.
  4. Require human review and evidence logs for material decisions.
  5. Monitor outcomes by stage, job family, geography, and group.

Your Leadership Role

You are not the Legal or Model Risk owner. You translate policy into operating controls, dashboards, workflows, training, and accountability.

You make AI safe enough, measurable enough, and operationally disciplined enough to scale.
